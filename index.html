Shenzhen Delighting Technology Co., Ltd. was founded in 2017 to become the most capable and reliable LED strip manufacturing partner in China. Our 21,000m² intelligent facility houses 30 fully automated SMT lines running 24/7 — delivering over 500,000 meters of premium LED strips daily.

We are a true manufacturer — R&D, PCB design, SMT assembly, silicone extrusion, injection molding, laser marking, aging and reliability testing, and packaging all under one roof. This vertical integration gives us complete control over quality, cost, and lead time.

Today DLT exports to 20+ countries — serving OEM/ODM brands, wholesale distributors, and retail chains with custom branding, bespoke packaging, and flexible MOQs.

Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before ordering.

What is your MOQ?

Our standard MOQ is 100 meters per model. For sample orders, we accept as little as 1–5 meters for evaluation.

How long is the production lead time?

Standard production takes 7–15 days depending on quantity and customization. Samples ship within 3–5 business days.

Do you offer OEM/ODM customization?

Yes — custom PCB, branding, packaging and specifications are all available. Just share your requirements and we will engineer it.

What certifications do you have?

Our products comply with EMC, FCC, RoHS, CE, LVD, SAA and ETL. Market-specific certifications can be arranged.

Can you help with logistics and shipping?

Yes — we ship by air, sea or express worldwide, and can arrange door-to-door delivery with your preferred forwarder.

What is your warranty policy?

LED strips carry 2–3 years warranty (depending on series), power supplies 5–7 years. Defective units are replaced free of charge.
